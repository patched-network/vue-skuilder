## intro start-welcome

**This is a skuilder study session**, running inside the docs site.

The course is static JSON served with these pages. Your answers, ratings and review schedule are
kept in this browser, and nothing is sent anywhere.

Questions about a docs page unlock once you've read that page, or once you've met its
introduction card here. Read a page, come back, and its questions are in play.

## card start-unit elo=900

In skuilder, the unit of study (usually the thing on screen) is called a {{card||page|lesson|deck}}.

## card start-card-type elo=940

A skuilder card type is a Vue component plus {{a class that grades responses||a JSON template|a CSS theme}}.

## card start-static elo=950

This demo has no backend. Course content is static JSON, and your learning history is stored {{in your browser||on a skuilder server|in a cookie on GitHub}}.

## card start-same-scale elo=980

Skuilder rates learners and cards on {{the same scale||separate, unrelated scales}}, so a card's difficulty can be compared directly with a learner's skill.

## card start-timebox elo=960

Study sessions are time-boxed: the learner chooses how long, and the system chooses {{what to show||how many cards are in the deck|the order of a fixed deck}}.

## card start-review-correct elo=930

After a correct answer, a card's review interval {{grows||resets}}.
